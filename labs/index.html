SC Labs

Where ideas become experiments.

Not every idea is ready to be a product. SC Labs is where we validate, prototype, and sometimes abandon ideas before they consume real resources. This is the work before the work.

How the Lab Works

Three rules. No exceptions.

Validate before investing

We test assumptions with minimal code before committing engineering resources. An experiment should answer one question clearly, then stop.

Abandon without guilt

A failed experiment is a successful learning. We kill ideas that don't prove out. The goal is evidence, not sunken-cost protection.

Graduate with evidence

An experiment graduates to a product only when it has proven the hypothesis — not when it feels promising. Evidence over intuition.
