The Market

Bolivia's rental market runs on word of mouth and handshakes.

Landlords do not verify tenants. Tenants cannot verify landlords. Agreements are informal. Disputes are ugly.

No Tenant Verification

Landlords rent to strangers based on gut feeling. Payment history, references, and identity are rarely checked.

No Listing Verification

Tenants browse listings with no guarantee they are real, accurate, or still available.

Informal Agreements

Most leases are handshake deals or loose paper contracts. When something goes wrong, there is nothing to stand on.

Bolivia ranks in the bottom quartile of Latin American countries for rental market transparency. Project Home is building the infrastructure that market needs.
