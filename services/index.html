Our Process

No surprises. No scope creep.

We are explicit about how we work before we work together. The process is the product.

01

Understand

Discovery call, brief review, and deep Q&A. We ask the uncomfortable questions before we quote — timeline, scope, edge cases, politics. We'd rather surface a problem now than promise something we can't deliver.

02

Scope

A clear scope document — fixed deliverables or a defined sprint structure, depending on the engagement. Agreed-upon communication cadence, decision-making protocol, and escalation path. You know what you're buying.

03

Build

Weekly progress updates. Async-first communication. You see everything as it happens — no big reveal at the end. If something needs to change, we flag it before it becomes a problem, not after.

04

Deliver

Full handoff with documentation. You own everything we build — source code, design files, deployment configurations, the works. Post-launch support window included. We don't disappear after go-live.

Honest Constraints

We're not the right team for everything.

Knowing when to say no is part of doing good work. These are genuine limits, not marketing.

We don't build without understanding. If you need execution before discovery — a quote before a conversation — we are not the right fit. We won't take a brief at face value without interrogating it first.

We don't do template-based websites. WordPress, Wix, Squarespace — these are valid tools for valid use cases. We don't compete with them, and we're not the right team if that's what you need.

We don't take on more than we can deliver. Our per-year capacity is intentionally limited. If we're at capacity when you reach out, we'll tell you — and we'll be honest about the wait.

We don't outsource. Everyone who touches your project is SC Web Studio. We will never subcontract your work to a third party without your knowledge and explicit agreement.
