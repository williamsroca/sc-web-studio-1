Case Assistant OS Active Development

An intelligent workstation for Personal Injury case management.

Manages the full case lifecycle from initial intake through medical records tracking, attorney coordination, and settlement. Built on a local-first architecture: your data stays on your machine, the system works offline, and nothing requires a cloud subscription.

Currently in active development with a small group of early-access PI firms. The goal: a system that fits the existing PI workflow without forcing firms to change how they operate.
